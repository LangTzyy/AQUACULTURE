The Three Sunken Chambers

Each door descends deeper into the AQUACULTURE. Not all who enter find their way back to the surface.

I

The Watching Deep

Sonar readings show movement in the caverns below — patterns too deliberate to be current, too large to be fish. Something down there knows you're here.

II

The Whispering Current

Divers report hearing voices carried by the deep ocean currents — words in no known language, growing clearer the deeper you go, then stopping. Silence is worse.

III

The Final Depth

No submarine has returned from the deepest chamber. The last transmission was a single word, repeated: "Opening." The door was already open when they arrived.

The Project

About The AQUACULTURE

An AR-powered web experience built to explore the mysteries of deep sea aquaculture.

Academic Project

Developed by Informatics Engineering students at Institut Teknologi & Bisnis Asia Malang as an exploration of Augmented Reality in marine science education.

AR Technology

Powered by A-Frame and AR.js, the experience brings deep-sea creatures to life through marker-based augmented reality — no app download required.

Try It Now

Point your camera at the provided markers to summon 3D creatures from the AQUACULTURE — a giant octopus, an AQUACULTURE fish, and a sunken anchor.
